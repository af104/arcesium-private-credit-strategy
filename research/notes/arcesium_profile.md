# Arcesium Profile (as of Nov 3, 2026): Business, Products, Clients, Private Credit Moves

## Business model, products/platform, asset classes, segments served

### Takeaway
Arcesium sells software (Opterra operations platform, Aquata data platform, Arcesium Intelligence AI layer, and, since Feb 2026, Limina's portfolio/order management system) plus Managed Services. It has been rebranding from a middle/back-office specialist into a "front-to-back" investment platform for hedge funds, asset managers, private markets firms, asset owners and sell-side firms.

### Cited Findings
- Jul 16, 2024: Arcesium packaged its existing tools as **Opterra**, a platform covering reconciliation, treasury and collateral, investor accounting, regulatory reporting and private-markets operations., [Wikipedia: Arcesium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcesium); [Arcesium newsroom](https://www.arcesium.com/newsroom); [AlternativesWatch, Jul 16 2024](https://www.alternativeswatch.com/2024/07/16/arcesium-launches-new-operational-platform-opterra/)

### Inferences
- The product sequence points to one platform strategy, with each layer added on top of the last:
  1. Data layer (Aquata, 2023)
  2. Operations layer (Opterra, 2024)
  3. Ecosystem (Marketplace, Oct 2025)
  4. AI (Dec 2025 features, then Arcesium Intelligence, May 2026)

- In private credit, Arcesium positions itself as the data, operations and fund-accounting system of record, not as a loan servicer or administrator. That is a natural "where to push next" question for the memo.

## Private credit specifics (Hypercore integration, lifecycle offerings, loan servicing, partnerships)

### Takeaway
Arcesium's private credit play is to be the governed data and fund-operations backbone. It partners with loan-administration specialists (Hypercore, Jun 2026) rather than doing servicing itself, and uses AI to automate loan notice and tape processing.

### Cited Findings
- **Jun 2, 2026: Hypercore integration**, announced by Hypercore from Tel Aviv via PR Newswire.
  - Hypercore is an AI loan administration company for private credit. Its products are a SaaS loan management system of record and an "AI Admin Agent" for post-close loan admin.
  - The integration moves loan-level data from origination through servicing and lifecycle events into Arcesium's platform in a structured, governed format.
  - That data then feeds NAV, fund accounting, LP reporting, reconciliation and regulatory compliance.
  - Division of labor: Hypercore handles loan operations; Arcesium handles fund operations and data infrastructure.
  - Quote from Hypercore CEO Daniel Liechtenstein: "Firms shouldn't have to rely on manual workarounds to manage critical workflows."
  - No joint clients were named.
 , [TMCnet/PR Newswire, Jun 2 2026](https://www.tmcnet.com/usubmit/-hypercore-announces-technology-integration-with-arcesium-comprehensive-private-/2026/06/02/10392783.htm); [FF News](https://ffnews.com/newsarticle/fintech/hypercore-announces-technology-integration-with-arcesium-for-comprehensive-private-credit-lifecycle-management/)
- **Dec 15, 2025: AI loan-notice extraction.** For private credit managers, the Aquata agent extracted loan lifecycle events (drawdowns, paydowns, interest repricing, fees) from notices sent by 15+ counterparties. Arcesium says this cut "hours of manual validation to minutes of exception-based review." Other use cases cited: direct lending borrower reporting, PE portfolio company financials, LP documents, real estate financing., [Business Wire, Dec 15 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251215394851/en)

## Partnerships, acquisitions, AI announcements, stated strategy

### Takeaway
Arcesium's stated strategy (2025 to 2026) is to be a unified front-to-back, data-centric and AI-native platform across public and private markets. Its main moves to get there: the Limina acquisition, the Aquata Marketplace ecosystem, the Hypercore integration, AWS Bedrock-based AI, and geographic expansion (Lisbon, Hong Kong, Stockholm).
