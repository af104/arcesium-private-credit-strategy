# Large Incumbent Competitors to Arcesium (Investment Ops / Middle and Back Office, Private Credit Focus), as of Nov 3, 2026

Scope: SS&C (Geneva, Advent, GlobeOp, Precision LM, loan agency), Clearwater Analytics (CWAN, incl. Enfusion, Beacon, Bistro), State Street Alpha (CRD, Mercatus/Alpha for Private Markets), BNY, FIS, Broadridge. Research done Nov 3, 2026 with roughly 24 search/fetch calls. Items marked [UNVERIFIED] come from background knowledge, were not confirmed by a source in this session, and should not be quoted without checking.

## 1. Asset class coverage, front-to-back reach, and delivery model

### Takeaway
SS&C and State Street are the two incumbents that combine software with outsourced fund administration across public and private credit; Clearwater has become the most direct SaaS rival to Arcesium after buying Enfusion (front office/OMS) and Beacon (cross-asset risk, incl. private credit) in 2025 and going private in June 2026. Broadridge (Sentry) and FIS (Private Capital Suite / Investran) are narrower, private-credit-PM or PE-accounting point solutions.

### Cited Findings
**SS&C**
- SS&C's private credit page lists: Precision LM ("loan origination, servicing and asset management platform"), Global Debt Manager (global agency/debt servicing), GlobeOp Fund Administration, Advent Geneva (multi-asset portfolio accounting and investor reporting), EVOLV (loan accounting, reserving, credit management), Investor Services, FundCentre (investor reporting) and DealCentre AI (page accessed Nov 3, 2026). [SS&C private credit page](https://ssctech.com/industry/private-markets/private-credit)
- Same page claims SS&C is the "#1" largest private equity fund administrator, with $830B in assets under administration and 500+ clients (private markets figures; undated page, accessed Nov 3, 2026). [SS&C private credit page](https://ssctech.com/industry/private-markets/private-credit)
- Delivery model: software licenses plus fund admin/BPO (GlobeOp). On the Q3 2026 call (Oct 22, 2026), management said owning the full fund-administration technology stack lets it deploy AI faster and has improved its win rate; it also said it would like to grow in fund administration but valuations there are very high. [GuruFocus via Yahoo Finance, Oct 23, 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ss-c-technologies-holdings-inc-030238243.html)
- Example of loan servicing win: Cauris selected SS&C as strategic loan servicing partner (PR Newswire; date per URL ID approx. 2022, not confirmed in this session). [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/cauris-selects-ssc-as-strategic-loan-servicing-partner-301539542.html)

**Clearwater Analytics (CWAN)**

[Clearwater press release](https://cwan.com/press-releases/clearwater-analytics-to-acquire-beacon-and-blackstones-bistro-to-deliver-investment-intelligence-across-private-markets/)
- Enfusion, acquired for $1.5B (announced Jan 2025), is a SaaS investment management platform (front office OMS/PMS plus accounting, hedge fund oriented). [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/clearwater-analytics-to-go-private-in-8-4bn-acquisition)
- Positioning at take-private close (Jun 25, 2026): "single, real-time view of every asset across public and private markets," clients trusting it with "over $10 trillion in assets globally." - Delivery model: pure SaaS, single-instance; core historic base is insurers and asset managers (e.g., Generali Deutschland and BarmeniaGothaer Asset Management wins in Q4 2025). [CWAN Q4 2025 release, Feb 18, 2026](https://www-web.itiger.com/news/2612429733)

[Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/pemberton-capital-advisors-adopting-broadridges-sentry-private-credit-portfolio-management-solution/amp/); [Mondo Visione](https://mondovisione.com/news/pemberton-capital-advisors-optimises-portfolio-management-with-broadridge-techno-202516); [IBS Intelligence (MUFG)](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/mufg-taps-broadridge-for-loan-tracking-reporting-tech); [Finextra (Northwall)](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/99928/northwall-capital-to-grow-credit-business-with-broadridges-sentry-loan-portfolio-management-system)

### Inferences
- Comparison grid (my synthesis of the above):
  - SS&C: public + private credit + PE + real assets; OMS (Eze, [UNVERIFIED]), accounting (Geneva), loan servicing/agency, fund admin, investor reporting; software + services + fund admin. Broadest overlap with Arcesium, and Arcesium historically ran on Geneva licensing.
  - Clearwater: public (deep in insurance), hedge funds via Enfusion, private credit risk/analytics via Beacon/Bistro; OMS/PMS, accounting, risk, reporting; pure SaaS. Most similar delivery model to Arcesium.
  - State Street: public via CRD, private via Alpha for Private Markets (Mercatus), plus fund admin; software + custody/admin bundle.
  - BNY: custody/admin plus Eagle data/accounting; private credit tooling not visible as a distinct product.
  - FIS: PE fund/partnership accounting and investor services (Investran lineage); weak private credit loan-level evidence.
  - Broadridge: private credit/CLO front-to-middle (Sentry); not a fund admin or full accounting book of record.
- Arcesium's differentiator against SS&C/State Street/BNY is neutrality (pure tech, no fund admin conflict); against Clearwater it is more about data platform depth and hedge fund / credit complexity.

## 2. Private credit specific capabilities and announcements 2024-2026

### Takeaway
The most material 2025-2026 private credit moves were Clearwater buying Beacon and Blackstone's Bistro (Mar 2025) and State Street building a daily NAV model for Apollo's private credit funds (Oct 2026). SS&C's private credit push shows up mainly through its admin scale and loan servicing stack rather than headline launches found in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Mar 12, 2025: Clearwater agreed to buy Beacon (~$560M; ~44M ARR end-2024; 60% cash, rest stock at ~$30.05/share) and Bistro from Blackstone ($125M; $10M cash, rest stock). The stated rationale is that investors are expanding private credit exposure and CIOs/CROs need public and private holdings, including private credit and structured products, in one view, with drill-down by company/industry/geography and cash flow modeling. [Clearwater press release](https://cwan.com/press-releases/clearwater-analytics-to-acquire-beacon-and-blackstones-bistro-to-deliver-investment-intelligence-across-private-markets/); [Alternative Credit Investor, Mar 12, 2025](https://alternativecreditinvestor.com/2025/03/12/clearwater-analytics-makes-private-credit-tech-acquisitions/)
- Beacon acquisition completion was reported (date not captured in this session). [FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/clearwater-analytics-completes-acquisition-of-beacon-deepening-risk-and-modeling-capabilities)
- Oct 28, 2026: State Street implemented a daily NAV operating model for certain private markets strategies to support expansion of "Daily Pricing" across Apollo's private credit business, converting a monthly valuation process into daily valuations and reporting for funds State Street administers. [State Street via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/state-street-advances-daily-nav-113000964.html)
- Related: Apollo wrote in May 2026 about private credit in an ETF wrapper (context for why daily NAV matters). [Apollo, May 2026](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/insights-news/insights/2026/05/private-credit-now-in-an-etf)

[IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/carlyle-taps-broadridge-sentry-to-manage-deal-pipeline-loan-operations); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/pemberton-capital-advisors-adopting-broadridges-sentry-private-credit-portfolio-management-solution/amp/)

### Inferences
- Retail/evergreen private credit (daily NAV, ETFs, interval funds) is becoming an operational battleground; administrators with valuation and NAV operations (State Street, SS&C, BNY) have a natural edge there over pure-software vendors, including Arcesium.
- Clearwater bought its way into private credit risk/analytics with a Blackstone-built tool, which gives it a large anchor-client reference in Arcesium's core target segment.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 private credit specific announcements found for BNY or FIS.
- No head-to-head loan-level data (e.g., loans serviced, private credit AUA) found for SS&C, State Street or BNY.

## 5. Positioning relative to Arcesium (overlap and head-to-head)

### Takeaway
SS&C has the deepest and most adversarial history with Arcesium (Geneva licensing dispute and litigation); Clearwater is now the closest like-for-like SaaS competitor in alternatives and private credit; State Street and BNY compete mainly where clients choose an outsourced admin-plus-tech bundle. No public head-to-head win/loss reports were found.

### Cited Findings
- Arcesium sued SS&C Advent alleging anticompetitive conduct over Geneva licensing (2020); SS&C called the allegations meritless. [Arcesium release, PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/arcesium-files-lawsuit-against-ssc-advent-for-illegal-and-anticompetitive-activities-301072814.html); [SS&C response](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/ssc-rejects-meritless-allegations-made-by-arcesium-301073078.html)
- Apr 1, 2021: SDNY Judge Mary Kay Vyskocil dismissed Arcesium's refusal-to-deal and exclusive-dealing antitrust claims, calling the alleged refusal an "unconsummated offer." [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/ss-c-ducks-more-antitrust-claims-over-geneva-accounting-software)
- SS&C separately sued Arcesium (SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. et al v. Arcesium LLC); a 2024 SDNY decision summary exists, but the outcome and current status were not confirmed in this session. [Patexia](https://services.patexia.com/lawsuits/SS%26C-Technologies-Holdings-Inc-et-al-v-Arcesium-LLC-id-165030); [Studicata 2024 summary](https://www.studicata.com/summaries/united-states-district-court-southern-district-of-new-york/ss-c-techs-holdings-v-arcsesium-llc-2024-i66yld/); [UniCourt](https://unicourt.com/case/pc-db5-ssc-technologies-holdings-inc-et-al-v-arcesium-llc-1154175)
- SS&C's client base includes "almost all of the large global macro funds" (Q3 2026 call), the same hedge fund segment Arcesium grew up in. [GuruFocus via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ss-c-technologies-holdings-inc-030238243.html)
- Clearwater/Beacon already counts Blackstone and PIMCO as Beacon users, and owns Blackstone's private credit tool Bistro (Mar 2025). [Clearwater](https://cwan.com/press-releases/clearwater-analytics-to-acquire-beacon-and-blackstones-bistro-to-deliver-investment-intelligence-across-private-markets/)
- WatersTechnology Waters Rankings 2025 lists category winners (not reviewed in detail; could show where Arcesium vs incumbents won awards). [WatersTechnology](https://www.waterstechnology.com/awards-rankings/7952557/waters-rankings-2025-all-the-winners-and-why-they-won)

### Inferences
- Overlap map (synthesis):
  - SS&C vs Arcesium: direct in hedge fund and credit accounting/ops and data; SS&C also sells admin, which Arcesium does not. Highest overlap.
  - Clearwater vs Arcesium: direct in SaaS front-to-back for alternatives (Enfusion) and private credit risk/analytics (Beacon/Bistro), plus insurance (where Arcesium has been pushing into insurers with private credit allocations; not verified here).
  - State Street Alpha vs Arcesium: overlap in data platform and private markets front-to-back for large asset managers; State Street wins when the client wants custody/admin bundled.
  - BNY vs Arcesium: overlap mostly in data management (Eagle) and outsourced middle office.
  - Broadridge Sentry vs Arcesium: overlap at private credit/CLO PM and loan ops for direct lenders; Sentry is narrower.
  - FIS vs Arcesium: limited; FIS is PE/fund accounting and investor services.
- The relationship between SS&C and Arcesium is partly adversarial (litigation) and partly dependency (Geneva); any update on that case matters for the landscape.

### Gaps
- No public head-to-head deal wins or losses between Arcesium and any of these six vendors were found.
- Status of SS&C v. Arcesium as of Nov 2026 is unknown; recommend a docket check.
- No source in this session confirmed Arcesium's own client list overlaps (e.g., Blackstone, Apollo); that belongs to the Arcesium profile research.
